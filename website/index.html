Field Theory’s official ChatGPT and Claude marketplace listings are not published yet. This preview supports a local plugin when supplied, or guided setup with the Onboard app. Your assistant’s computer-use feature must be available on your plan.

One conversation. A few necessary approvals.

  1. Check your Mac. Reuse apps, accounts, and permissions that already work.
  2. Prepare Field Theory. Sign in securely and set up local transcription.
  3. Allow recording and computer use. Your assistant explains each approval in Mac settings.
  4. Try it on a call. Record your helper, check the transcript, and test a simple computer action.

Prefer an app to handle installation?

Onboard checks your Mac and installs missing tools. Continue in your assistant for permissions and the final test.
